Doppa Privacy Policy

Version 2 · Effective November 27, 2026

Doppa (小日友 in Chinese, "the game") is operated by Doppa Team (operated by YEN TING-YA) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and how you can manage your data.

1. What we collect

Category What Source
Sign-in data The account identifier from Apple or Google, and the email address Apple or Google shares if you allow it. Guest sign-in uses a randomly generated device identifier. We never receive your Apple or Google password. Apple, Google, your device
Profile Nickname, previous nicknames, character look and outfits, avatar expression, bio You
Game data Room layout, pets, inventory, coin and gem balances, quest and achievement progress, focus timer sessions, race records, NPC friendship levels Your play
Weather settings The city you choose (city name, region, country and the coordinates of the city center), and whether "real weather outside my window" is on You search for and choose it
Location Approximate location (about 1 km), only if you turn on "Use my location for weather". The server stores only the coordinates rounded to 2 decimal places and the matching city name Your device (with your permission)
Social content Friends and block list, chat and direct messages, guestbook notes, mail, room visits Your interactions with other players
Safety and moderation Reports (including a copy of the reported message, name and room), warnings, mutes and bans, which version of the terms you accepted and when Reports and our review
Technical data IP address, connection times, app version, device OS type, error logs Recorded automatically by our servers

We do not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or health data. The game does not access your device's location unless you turn on "Use my location for weather", and even then only your approximate location (see "4. Location"). You can also search for and pick a city yourself. Choosing one is optional; by default the game shows the weather in Taipei. The game currently has no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How we use it

  1. To sign you in and save and sync your progress.
  2. To run multiplayer features such as friends, chat, room visits and mini-game matches.
  3. To filter inappropriate words, handle reports, enforce our Terms of Use and keep the community safe.
  4. To prevent cheating, scams and abuse.
  5. To fix bugs and keep the servers stable.
  6. To show real weather outside your room's window and in town, based on the city you choose or, if you turn on "Use my location for weather", your approximate location.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

  • Other players: your nickname, previous nicknames, character, room, guestbook and messages you post in public chat. Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation, except when reported (see below).
  • Our moderators: only the content involved when handling a report.
  • Service providers: our servers run on Oracle Cloud in Tokyo, Japan, which stores data on our behalf. Sign-in is verified by Apple and Google.
  • Weather data provider: we use OpenWeather (OpenWeather Ltd., United Kingdom) for weather data. To get the weather, our server sends OpenWeather only the city's coordinates, rounded to 2 decimal places (about 1 km). When you search for a city, it sends the text you typed and your game language. These requests come from our server and do not include your account, nickname or IP address. Weather for each place is cached on our server and shared by all players, without recording who asked.
  • Players visiting your room: see the weather outside your window, but not the name of the city you chose.
  • Legal requests: we may disclose necessary data when required by law.

4. Location

  • The game asks for location permission and reads your approximate location only if you turn on "Use my location for weather" in the weather panel. If you don't, it never reads your location.
  • Accuracy is about 1 km: on iOS the game uses reduced (approximate) accuracy, and on Android it asks only for ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION. The game does not ask for precise location.
  • Location is requested at most once each time you open the app, and only while the app is open. The game never reads your location in the background.
  • Your location is not used for tracking or for ads.
  • Our server stores only the coordinates rounded to 2 decimal places (about 1 km) and the matching city name, and uses them only to show your local weather.
  • Your location and city name are not shown to other players.
  • You can turn this off at any time in the weather panel or in your device's settings. Weather then uses the city you chose yourself.

5. Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers in Tokyo, Japan. If you live outside Japan, your data is transferred to Japan. We may later move servers to another region (for example Taiwan) and will update this policy if we do.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and game data: until you delete your account.
  • Report and sanction records: 1 year after the case is closed, to handle appeals and repeat abuse.
  • After an account is deleted, reports other players made about that account (including the content snapshot taken at the time) are kept for safety review for up to 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Server technical logs: up to 90 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  1. View and change your nickname, character and room in the game.
  2. Delete your account in the game under Menu › Settings › Delete account. We delete your account and game data within 30 days, except reports other players made about you (including the content snapshot taken at the time), which are kept for safety review for up to 30 days and then deleted automatically.
  3. Email us to access, copy, correct, restrict or delete your personal data. We reply within 30 days.

You can use these options to exercise your rights under laws such as Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, Japan's APPI, the EU GDPR and California's CCPA.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children. Players under 13 need a parent or guardian's permission. If a parent believes their child gave us personal data without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted (TLS), servers expose only the ports they need, and the admin console requires a login. No method of transmission is completely secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

10. Changes

When this policy changes we update the effective date on this page, and we tell you in the game about significant changes.
