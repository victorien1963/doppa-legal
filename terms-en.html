Doppa Terms of Use and Community Rules

Version 1 · Effective November 25, 2026

Welcome to Doppa! So everyone can decorate and make friends safely, please read and agree to these terms before playing.

Zero tolerance

Doppa has zero tolerance for the following. Content will be removed, and accounts may be muted or permanently banned:

  1. Harassing, bullying, threatening or stalking other players.
  2. Sexual content or sexual harassment, or any sexual content involving minors.
  3. Hate speech targeting race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar.
  4. Encouraging self-harm, suicide or violence.
  5. Scams, off-game trading, or asking for personal information or passwords.
  6. Spam, ads or harmful links.
  7. Pretending to be another player or staff.
  8. Cheats, exploits or any other cheating.

These rules apply to everything you create or share in the game: chat, messages, names, bios, notes and rooms.

Reporting and blocking

  1. To report something, press and hold the message or tap "⋯" on the player's profile, then choose "Report". The player is not told.
  2. We review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks the rules, and warn, mute or ban the player.
  3. You can block any player at any time. Blocked players can't see your messages or visit your room, and you can't see theirs. Manage your list in Friends › Blocked.
  4. False or abusive reports also break the rules.

Your content

  1. You are responsible for the names, text and rooms you create.
  2. To handle reports we keep a copy of reported content, used only for review and community safety.
  3. We filter inappropriate words automatically, but can't catch everything. Please report what gets through.

Your account

  1. Create an account only for yourself. If you are under 13, play with a parent or guardian's permission.
  2. Keep your Apple or Google account safe. You are responsible for what happens through it.
  3. If you break these terms we may suspend or close your account. Virtual items are not refunded except where the law requires.

Virtual items

Coins, points, gems, furniture, outfits and other virtual items can only be used in the game. They can't be exchanged for money or traded outside the game.

Changes

When these terms change we'll tell you in the game and ask you to agree again.
